Privacy Policy

Application: Snake Rush: Retro Arcade
Last Updated: November 22, 2026
Summary: Snake Rush: Retro Arcade is designed to respect your privacy. We do not collect, sell, or store any personal information. All gameplay data is stored strictly on your local device. Minimal anonymous data is processed by Google AdMob exclusively to deliver banner advertisements.

1. Information Collection & Usage

We do not require account registration, and we do not collect personal identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, phone number, contacts, or precise location.

Gameplay data (such as your high score, current score, and game speed preferences) is stored locally on your device via internal app storage and is never transmitted to external servers.

2. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the game free to play for everyone, our app integrates Google AdMob to display banner ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic information to serve ads, including:

  • Standard device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • Device hardware model and operating system version.
  • IP address (used to infer general non-precise geographic region).
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, and load performance).

For more details on how Google handles advertising data and how you can opt out of personalized ads, please visit Google's Privacy & Terms.

3. Device Permissions

The app requests minimal device permissions strictly required for core functionality:

  • INTERNET (android.permission.INTERNET): Required exclusively to fetch and display banner ads from Google AdMob servers.

The app does not access your camera, microphone, storage, contacts, location, or background processes.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Our game is a casual arcade title suitable for players of all ages, including families and children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 years of age. All ad requests follow child-directed and family-friendly policy guidelines.

5. Security

Because no personal data is collected or maintained on external servers, your personal data remains completely safe on your device. Third-party ad transmissions are encrypted and protected under Google's industry-leading security standards.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be updated on this page with a revised "Last Updated" date.
